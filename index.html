About

Most of what I make rebuilds something old, like a record deck or a Windows 9x desktop, with what a browser can do now. Nearly all of it runs on your own device: no account, no server, and your files stay where they are. Lately that has grown to terminal tools, private browsers and AI models that run on your own computer.

Several projects are forks. I took an open-source project I liked and kept adding to it, and each one names whose work it started from.
