About

Most of what I make rebuilds something old, like a record deck or a Windows 9x desktop, with what a browser can do now. Nearly all of it runs on your own device: no account, no server, and your files stay where they are.

Several projects are forks. I took an open-source project I liked and kept adding to it, and each one names whose work it started from.

l.a.vandeweerd@gmail.com
github.com/Gitguy268

The glass at the top runs the shaders of Liquid Glass Studio by Charles Yin. The type is Anybody and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. This page makes no network requests beyond its own files.

The docs explain how every project works. Source of this page, MIT licence.
